## What changed

- Completed the end-to-end damage and healing flow across Health, Damage & Stress, Conditions, and Attribute Damage worlds, including both dice-pool and stepped-dice variants.
- Corrected pushed-roll Conditions so the standard push cost and Attribute-bane damage apply the proper number of distinct Conditions without silently losing or duplicating consequences.
- Made Shift recovery atomic, prevented healthy Actors from producing no-op recovery messages, and preserved all choices when recovery affects more than one category.
- Added SRD-accurate treatment timing for lethal Critical Injuries, including Round-to-Stretch-to-Shift stabilization, failed-treatment locks, repeated death saves, and in-world treatment durations.
- Prevented unstabilized lethal injuries from disappearing when their healing-day countdown ends, and made edited injury fields safely rebuild stale healing and death-save timers.
- Automated the special treatment rules for Sprained Ankle, Blood in Eyes, and Cracked Spine, including permanent paralysis when Cracked Spine is not treated before its deadline.
- Added migration for existing Critical Injuries and rebuilt the SRD Items compendium with the new treatment rules.
- Added an automated regression suite covering every damage model, both Attribute Damage dice variants, Conditions cancellation, push consequences, timed healing, and lethal stabilization.
- Combined Manoeuvres and Harm & Recovery into a responsive Actions panel, with Harm & Recovery arranged as a compact two-column control grid.
- Removed the redundant Equipped Items summary from the General tab while retaining full equipment management in Inventory.
- Compacted Weapon Item fields and corrected every Item Description tab so it uses the available window space without unnecessary outer scrolling.
